Post-launch review dashboard

Release management and post-release data analysis for product managers. Every release below declared its hypothesis and success metrics up front; the dashboard scores observed data against those targets.

How scoring works

For each metric, the observed value is the mean of readings inside the measurement window (ship date through ship date + window days; readings outside the window are ignored).

% of target achieved: observed / target × 100 for increase metrics, target / observed × 100 for decrease metrics. Verdicts: hit at 100%+, marginal at 90–99.9%, miss below 90%. A release scores the mean of its metrics; its verdict is hit only if every metric hit.
